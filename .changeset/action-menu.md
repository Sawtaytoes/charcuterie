Add ActionMenu, the overflow control: a ghost neutral `IconButton` that opens a `Menu` at `bottom-end`, with its own open state. Props are `items`, `label` (the trigger's accessible name, which also names the menu), an optional `size`, and an optional `icon`. The default icon is three dots drawn in CSS, not a glyph or an SVG.

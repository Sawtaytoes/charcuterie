Add `CatalogPicker`, a picker for one value out of a large catalog that falls into groups,
such as AI models by provider and account. A trigger shows the chosen item's mark and label.
The panel has a vertical rail (Favorites, then one tab per group) beside a search field over
the list; typing searches every group at once, under group headings. Exports
`CatalogPicker`, `CatalogPickerProps`, `CatalogPickerGroup`, and `CatalogPickerItem`.

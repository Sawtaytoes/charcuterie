`LaneTimeline` wraps a bar's title onto up to three lines once a day column is at least `wrapColumnSize` wide (default 6em of the timeline's own type), and clips to one line with an ellipsis below that. The title is `text-sm` in every shape, and every bar in a packed row is the height of the tallest. A week across a desktop page printed `Regional Rob…` at 7em a column.

Six is what one pixel can be, not what the panel can show. Every swatch below is read out of packages/tokens/src/epaper.ts. The two that do not survive

pairauthored midpointwhat the panel puts up instead
black + yellow #747012 green and red both sit closer to the midpoint than either parent does, so the panel puts up a red/green checkerboard instead — which is redGreen, a blend that already exists.
yellow + blue #847F6A the midpoint is chroma 26, exactly the neutral guard, so castkit dithers it against black and white only. Nudging the chroma past the guard does not help: green and red are still nearer than yellow or blue, and the result becomes a three-ink mush.

Both are absent from spectra6Blends rather than present and wrong. A token that silently renders as a different pair is worse than one that does not exist.

Why the six-ink rule still holds for small geometry

“While more complex algorithms like Rotated Bayer or Error Diffusion are excellent for photographic gradients, they often struggle with small-scale graphical primitives … introduced noticeable geometric artifacts that distracted from the clean lines of the UI elements.” — Beyond 6 Colors. A 1px rule made of a 1px checkerboard is half a line; small text made of one is a smear.

A large fill in yellowRed — what blends are for. The 1px checkerboard has room to average, and the eye receives the mix.

The same colour as a 1px rule and as 11px text — what it is not for. One row of pixels leaves the dither nowhere to go.

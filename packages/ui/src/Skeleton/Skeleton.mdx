reflow when the images land, and layout shift is what users actually notice.

## `aria-hidden` is the contract, not an optimisation

A skeleton is decoration standing in for content that does not exist yet. Announcing it
means a screen reader reading three empty bars, and an agent finding a "loading" element it
cannot act on.

**The load has to be announced by the region that owns it** — a `Spinner`, or `aria-busy`
on the container. The `Default` story asserts the skeleton is hidden from assistive
technology, and `Interactive` asserts it contributes no roles at all. Both are assertions
axe cannot make for you: there is nothing invalid about a visible placeholder.

# ProgressBar

Five repos hand-roll one and **only rip-deck's has `role="progressbar"`**. The other four
are a `<div>` with a percentage width: invisible to assistive technology and to
`getByRole("progressbar")`.

This is the union — rip-deck's ARIA and its indeterminate sweep, plus threshold colours
nobody has yet.

## Three corrections to rip-deck's version

1. **The role goes on the track, not the fill.** rip-deck puts `role="progressbar"` on the
   filled element, so a 4% rip is a progressbar 4px wide as far as AT and a Playwright
   bounding box are concerned.
2. **The name comes from a real label**, wired with `aria-labelledby` — visible or
   `VisuallyHidden` — rather than a hardcoded `aria-label` that cannot be translated.
3. **`aria-valuenow` is omitted while indeterminate**, which is what says "unknown" rather
   than "zero".

## Indeterminate means "no measurable progress yet"

Not zero, and not unknown-therefore-full. rip-deck's case is the AACS/BD+ preamble: ~25 s
of a real Blu-ray emitting nothing, where a full bar reads as a finished rip and an empty
one as a wedged drive. The sweep animates `inset-inline-start`, so it runs the other way in
RTL for free, and its reduced-motion fallback hatches the whole track rather than parking a
bar at one end.

## Values arrive from a log parser

`-1`, `101`, `NaN`, and `0 of 0` are all normal in this fleet, so clamping is a tested pure
function (`progressValue.ts`) and `aria-valuenow` is clamped in the value's own units —
never disagreeing with what is drawn.

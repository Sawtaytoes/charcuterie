# EmailViewer

Mail Sifter's email viewer, moved here so every app that shows Mail Sifter's mail renders it
the same way. Starchart's focus window is the second consumer: it shows an email with the
owning app's own viewer instead of a copy of it.

<Canvas of={EmailViewerStories.Newsletter} />

## The walls

The HTML is **already sanitized** by Mail Sifter's server (`sanitize-html` plus its link and
image annotator). The viewer never sanitizes, and must never be handed HTML that did not
come through that sanitizer. It is the second and third wall:

- a sandboxed `<iframe>` with **no `allow-same-origin`**, so the message has an opaque origin;
- a `<meta>` policy of `default-src 'none'`, where the only script allowed is one
  app-authored scroll observer, approved by its SHA-256 hash;
- `referrerpolicy="no-referrer"` on the frame and inside the document.

## Images

`hasLoadedImages` is the parent's decision, never the viewer's. `shouldLoadRemoteImages` is
Mail Sifter's policy: ordinary mail loads its remote images, and mail with a link warning
keeps them parked unless the sender is trusted. When images load, the policy widens exactly
one directive, `img-src`, to `data: https: http:` — the browser then fetches each image
from the sender's own server, which tells the sender the mail was opened. Inline `cid:`
images arrive as `data:` URLs and render either way.

Render `EmailImageNotice` **above** the viewer for warned mail, never inside it.

## It fits its container

The frame measures its own width, not the window's, so a docked drawer or a narrow panel
gets a message scaled to fit (`isScaledToFit`) rather than a sideways scrollbar.

<Canvas of={EmailViewerStories.ScaledToFit} />

## The masthead

App chrome — a subject, a sender row — goes in `masthead`. It stays in the app's document,
out of reach of email CSS; the frame reserves its measured height and slides it away as the
message scrolls.

## Attachments

`EmailAttachmentList` renders **nothing** when `attachments` is absent or empty, so an app
whose source does not send attachment data yet passes what it has. Without `onDownload` the
rows are a plain list; with it, each row is a button that runs the app's own signed request.
Its columns come from its container: one, then two from `cq-md`, then three from `cq-xl`.
